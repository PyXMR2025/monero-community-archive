### My Commentary

I tried to capture the headliner items as neutrally as possible. Obviously you can see some of my own bias in the snippets though. For full context, please read the room log.

In many ways, I think the chat/summary speaks for itself. The nature and timing of how this went down is odd. Questions I have:
- If the dream of a non-profit MoneroKon was so important, shouldn't it have been brought to the general community *before* taking a vote? And shouldn't Twisted Edwards have organized an event this year, even if in cooperation with other organizations?
- Why would a vote on MK27 venue be done *before* voting on the next leader?
- Is it acceptable for a private Nextcloud instance to conduct an anonymous vote for something as important as venue?
- Who are the individuals in @ajs-xmr DMs? Do they overlap with the Nextcloud voters? Are they upset about the venue still being in question and not decided by a potentially dubious vote?

Overall I think @orangefren did a great job with the conference this year, especially under time constraints. I also respect the significant contributions @ajs-xmr has made. I even respect the notion that, perhaps a broader community discussion should be had. For example, is there a question over the branding, trademarks, domains, and social media? Does it make sense for @orangefren's for-profit LLC to disclaim exclusive commercial rights over these items, in case conference organization should change hands again in the future?

But it's hard to argue with the fact that @orangefren was willing to step up to carry the torch when Twisted Edwards declined to do so. I don't think that *non-profit* vs *for-profit* is the important question here. I think it's that the *.."IP"..* remain non-exclusively owned by any corporate entity, even if stewarship of control is transferred.
