## ✅ Collaboration with the Bitcoin Film Fest and Revolution.rocks music festival
This proposal outlines hosting MK7 in Warsaw, following the successful blueprint of MK6 but including a partnership with a music festival. Similar to MK6, I suggest sharing only the reception area with the BFF at most. We would honor each other's tickets, granting MK attendees access to all BFF screenings, workshops, **daily parties**, and concerts at Revolution.rocks.

Warsaw beats Prague venues:
* Located in the Polish equivalent of Times Square, but paired with a park.
* Access to no-KYC Bitcoin ATMs (capped at €1,000 per transaction, unfound elsewhere in the EU).
* Forgiving drug-policing in practice.
* Just one crosswalk away from 2, 3, 4, and 5-star hotels, multiple shopping centers, and Poland’s busiest train station (offering direct daily connections to Berlin, Prague, Vilnius, and elsewhere).
* Closer to the airport than the proposed Prague venues are to Prague's city center.
* Warsaw remains considerably cheaper than Prague, features superior restaurants (as noted by TheFuzzStone), and we have established, trusted relationships with local suppliers (including our superb MK6 AV and booth teams).

Benefits of collaborating:
* Fresh networking opportunities for attendees and significantly larger, higher-value crowds for our sponsors.
* Instead of just adding a standalone movie screening or a closing concert, attendees get full access to a film and music festival.
* Near-continuous activity (9:00 AM – ~3:00 AM) made possible by multiple organizing teams working in rolling shifts.
* Drastically lower costs per attendee (e.g., shared liability insurance) and significantly higher negotiating power with venues and vendors.

## Venues in Prague

**I am the only person to have went to Prague to check out the venues proposed** so here's some observations.

I've seen only one venue in the city which meets the minimum requirements for a MoneroKon. If MK has to be in Prague I'll organise it in:
### 🆗 Pragovka
This is freshly renovated warehouse.

* A bit tight, but manageable. It has just enough space for sponsor booths and workshops.
* Features a nearby café serving alcohol, full AC, and an acceptable (though barely green) outdoor area.
* No interior CCTV. Located directly across the road from a shooting range.
* Near the yellow (B) metro line, but still a 30-minute commute from the city center.

### ❌ Other (unsuitable!) venues
❌ **Na Výstavišti (New Spiral):**

- The audience wraps around the central stage across three elevated spirals. This presents massive production challenges: recording the speaker without filming the audience is nearly impossible (as pointed out by hbs), setting up projection screens is a logistical nightmare, and Q&A mic-running is totally impractical. Just finding a path to sit near your friends after spotting them is tricky.
- For anyone with stage fright, being surrounded 360-degree from above and below is incredibly intimidating. The layout is built for long-form theater, not 20 minute talks.
- No proper space for sponsor booths, barely any outdoor seating, and the venue manager admitted the climate control is "more of an air circulation system, not actual AC." It's also highly inconvenient to get to.

❌ **MeetFactory:**

* The designated "relaxing outdoor space" sits directly underneath a busy highway. 
* The venue is entirely too small. There is no room for a secondary workshop stage or sponsor booths.
* The venue operators explicitly stated that the AC is too loud to run while speakers are talking. 
* Deeply inconvenient. My Uber driver couldn't navigate the roads and literally suggested I get out and walk across four sets of active railroad tracks.

❌ **Stará čistírna - Old water treatment plant:**

- The venue is too small, structurally rundown, and lacks AC.
- While it boasts a large outdoor footprint, the entire perimeter suffers from an unbearable, persistent stench of raw sewage.
- There is no layout option that allows sponsor booths to sit naturally in the path of attendee foot traffic.
- Not convenient to get to

❌ **Divadlo X10:**

- They take a TWO MONTH holiday for summer
