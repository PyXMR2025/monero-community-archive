# Original Description
Ok as you guys know, ZEC recently, despite being open source, and so on, allowed for a vulnerability where unlimited coins could be forged and exploitation may have never been detected or proven.

I propose XMR or the Monero project shall start the research of implementing following things:

* Being able to give people a rough idea of supply distribution while preserving privacy / non-traceability relative to any single entity

* Any further mechanisms or ideas to detect or at least hint at whether such behavior could be occurring on Monero, with cryptographic proof.

If this problem is solved, it would make XMR truly the better BTC.

I know this is difficult, because these properties contradict the idea of privacy, but research on it should be prioritized to say the least.

Monero already does many things better than ZEC:

* It's 'simpler', meaning it's easier to verify
* Since cryptographic commitments are still fully validated by every node, it has an always-auditable supply state
* No hidden pools whose internal consistency must be separately trusted, unlike ZEC.


But I still think the addition of a way to prove rough supply distribution while preserving privacy, as contradictory as it may seem first, must be at least under research...
