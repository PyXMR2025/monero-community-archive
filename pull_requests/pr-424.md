# Original Description
At a high-level view, this PR refactors selfsend proposals to replace $D^{other}_e$ with $d^{other}_e$ and $\text{is\\_subaddress}$. $D_e$ is then generated dynamically at output finalization time such that the discrete log of $D_e$ is $d_e$ or $d^{other}_e$, against either $G$ or the applicable $K^j_s$, depending on that context. This lets users generate OutProofs to special enotes in all >2-out txs, or to 1 special enote in a 2-out, 2-selfsend tx (by convention the one marked "PAYMENT"), to match current `wallet2` capabilities.
