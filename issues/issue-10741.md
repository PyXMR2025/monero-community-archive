# Original Description
Environment: Linux, Kubuntu 26.04 LTS
Wallet: Monero Wallet Gui 0.18.5.0 binary for Linux from getmonero.org 
Hardware Wallet Used: Ledger Nano S Plus, Monero app up-to-date with ledger live

## Problem has been observed as following:

1. Enter in destination address
2. Select all balance
3. Select fee amount (Lowest)
4. Send
5. The ledger will receive the TX for a few seconds and try to process it however it will disconnect (unrelated to cable issue, i tried multiple cables it is not about the physical connection). Possibly related to an internal bug or a panic and disconnecting itself. However it won't turn off.

## What to know

- I haven't tried alternative accounts or different Ledger devices
- My ledger firmware is up-to-date and nothing is tampered with.

## Guesses

- It may be related to dusting attacks, because entering a manual amount very close to the complete amount works just fine. If selecting (all) chooses an unspendable output to include ledger may panic and disconnect(maybe?)
- I'd need to test with a temporary account using a passphrase in Ledger to make sure. I will do it and update the issue soon when I am free.
- In any case if it is related to a dusting attack or similar, or something else, it's good to research this.
